REBORN

A life points tracker for Yu-Gi-Oh! duels, quick at the table and light on the battery.

What it does

  • Changes life points with one tap, with an add mode for gains.
  • Keeps a history for each player, where any earlier value can be restored.
  • Gives each player a calculator, a coin and a die.
  • Supports 1v1 duels and Tag Duels, with TCG and OCG starting values.
  • Offers four display modes for the different ways of placing the phone.
  • Lets each player choose a name, a color and a background pattern.
  • Works fully offline.

Questions

Does REBORN need an internet connection?

No. The app works entirely on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I start a new duel?

Tap Reset in the middle of the screen and confirm.

I entered a wrong value. How do I fix it?

Tap the player's life points to open the history, then tap Apply on the entry you want to go back to.
