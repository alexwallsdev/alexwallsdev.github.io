REBORN

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains how REBORN - Life Points Tracker ("REBORN") handles information. REBORN is developed by Alex Walls.

In short

REBORN collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Match data: life points, the history of changes and the match settings.
  • Preferences: display mode, haptics and screen settings, and each player's name, color and pattern.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Reset match clears the current match, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What the app collects

Nothing. REBORN has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Device features

REBORN uses vibration for tap feedback and keeps the screen awake during a match. Both can be turned off in the app's settings. The app does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

App stores

Google Play and the App Store may share aggregated statistics with the developer, such as the number of installs and crash reports, under their own privacy policies and your device settings. These statistics do not identify you.

Children

Since REBORN collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how REBORN handles information, for example by adding advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
